[Koala](/koala/) is my app for training one BJJ goal at a time. A new build is out on TestFlight and on the Google Play closed beta. Here is what changed.

## One answer a day

After class you tell Koala if you pulled off your goal: yes or no. That's it, once a day. If something clicked, you can add a note.

## A page for each goal

Tap a goal and you see every day you logged it, and where it stands.

## Ideas by belt

Not sure what to work on next? Koala now has goal ideas grouped by belt. Add one to Up next with a tap, and undo it if you change your mind. The ideas are starting points, not lessons: what to drill is between you and your coach.

## How Koala works

New users get a short checklist that walks them through the basics, including how to save a goal for later.

## Try it

Koala is free, with no account and no tracking. Get it on [the Koala page](/koala/).
